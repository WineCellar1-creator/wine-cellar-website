From fruit to bottle

the whole process, one app

Real screens from the app, in the order you will meet them.

01

Pick a recipe that fits your fruit

Choose a built-in recipe or write your own. Enter how much fruit you have and pick dry, semi-sweet or sweet. Wine Cellar scales the sugar, water and syrup doses for you.

  • Natural fruit sugar is included in the calculation
  • Estimated wine volume and fermenter size up front

02

Every batch gets its own jar

Each wine sits on a shelf in your cellar with its volume, start date, stage and readings. Open the jar to see exactly where that batch stands.

  • OG, SG and ABV recorded per batch
  • Taste-test notes and a photo diary

03

Never miss a racking day

The calendar plans each step for every batch. You get a reminder when it is time to measure, rack, add syrup or bottle.

  • One calendar for all your batches
  • A home-screen widget shows today's tasks

04

Wine Lab+ does the maths

Calculators for OG and FG to ABV, SG, Brix and °Blg, temperature correction and sugar additions. Every term has a ? that explains it.

  • Hydrometer temperature correction
  • A bottling-readiness check based on stable SG

05

Learn as you go

Short step-by-step guides for the hydrometer, racking, filtration, sanitation and bottling, plus an equipment checklist. Useful for your first batch and your fiftieth.

Good to know

Who is Wine Cellar for?

Adult hobby winemakers who make fruit or grape wine at home and want to keep track of their batches. It suits beginners, thanks to the guides, and people with many jars going at once.

Do I need special equipment?

A hydrometer helps you get the most from the app, because readings drive the ABV and bottling-readiness checks. The equipment checklist in the guide lists the rest.

How much does it cost?

It is a one-time purchase on Google Play. The current price in your country is shown on the store page.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not at the moment. Wine Cellar is available for Android phones and tablets.

Where can I get help?

Write to supportwinecellar1@gmail.com. The tutorial can be replayed any time from Settings.
