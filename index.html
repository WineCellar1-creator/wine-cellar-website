How it works

From fruit to bottle

Real screens from the app, in the order you will use them.

  1. 01

    Pick a recipe that fits your fruit

    Choose a built-in recipe or write your own. Enter how much fruit you have and pick dry, semi-sweet or sweet; Wine Cellar scales the sugar, water and syrup doses.

    • Natural fruit sugar included in the calculation
    • Estimated wine volume and fermenter size up front
  2. 02

    Every batch gets its own jar

    Each wine sits on its own shelf with its volume, start date, stage and readings. Open the jar to see exactly where that batch stands.

    • OG, SG and ABV recorded per batch
    • Taste-test notes and a photo diary
  3. 03

    Never miss a racking day

    The calendar plans every step for every batch and reminds you when it is time to measure, rack, add syrup or bottle.

    • One calendar for all your batches
    • A home-screen widget with today’s tasks
  4. 04

    Wine Lab+ does the maths

    Calculators for OG and FG to ABV, SG, Brix and °Blg, temperature correction and sugar additions. Every term has a ? that explains it.

    • Hydrometer temperature correction
    • Bottling-readiness check based on stable SG
  5. 05

    Learn as you go

    Step-by-step guides for the hydrometer, racking, filtration, sanitation and bottling, plus an equipment checklist.

    • Written for beginners, useful for experienced makers
    • Glossary of OG, SG, FG, ABV, °Bx and ΔSG

Good to know

Who is Wine Cellar for?

Adult hobby winemakers who make fruit or grape wine at home and want to keep track of their batches. Beginners get guides; experienced makers get one place for many batches.

Do I need special equipment?

A hydrometer lets you use the app fully, because readings drive the ABV and bottling-readiness checks. The equipment checklist lists the rest.

How much does it cost?

It is a one-time purchase on Google Play, with no subscription and no ads. The price in your country is shown on the store page.

Which languages are supported?

English, Polish, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian and Czech. You can switch language in Settings at any time.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not at the moment. Wine Cellar is available for Android phones and tablets.

Where can I get help?

Write to supportwinecellar1@gmail.com. The tutorial can be replayed any time from Settings.
